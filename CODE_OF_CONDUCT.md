# Code of conduct

[中文](#行為準則)

Saturn adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)** as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone taking part in the project: in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and any other project space, and when representing the project elsewhere.

In short: be welcoming and respectful, accept constructive criticism gracefully, focus on what is best for the community, and do not harass, insult, demean or threaten anyone, or publish others' private information. The full text linked above is what applies.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at **[security@saturn-core.com](mailto:security@saturn-core.com)** (put "Code of conduct" in the subject). Reports are read only by the maintainers and handled confidentially; you will get an answer, and the person reported will not learn who reported them unless you agree.

## Enforcement

The maintainers ([`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md)) review each report and decide on a response following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project's spaces, depending on the severity and whether it is repeated. They may remove or edit comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code.

# 行為準則

[English](#code-of-conduct)

Saturn 採用 **[Contributor Covenant 2.1 版](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)** 作為行為準則。它適用於參與專案的每一個人：在 issue、pull request、審查、討論與專案的任何其他空間，以及在其他場合代表專案時。

簡言之：友善而尊重地對待他人，虛心接受建設性的批評，以社群的最佳利益為重，不騷擾、侮辱、貶低或威脅任何人，也不公開他人的私人資訊。實際適用的是上方連結的完整條文。

## 回報

不當行為請寄至 **[security@saturn-core.com](mailto:security@saturn-core.com)** 回報給維護者（主旨請註明「Code of conduct」）。回報只有維護者會閱讀並保密處理；你會收到回覆，除非你同意，被回報者不會知道是誰回報的。

## 執行

維護者（見 [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md)）會審視每一份回報，並依 Contributor Covenant 的執行準則決定處理方式：視嚴重程度與是否重複發生，給予私下提醒、警告、暫時禁止或永久禁止參與專案空間。維護者可以移除或編輯違反本準則的留言、commit、issue 與其他貢獻。
